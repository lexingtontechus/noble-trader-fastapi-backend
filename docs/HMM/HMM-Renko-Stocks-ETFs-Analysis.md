## Methodology

1. **Data**: Daily close prices from Yahoo Finance (~514 bars, ~2 years)
2. **Renko Construction**: Fixed brick size mode, reversal=2 bricks
3. **Signal Generation**: Bull/bear trigger patterns with regime gate enabled
4. **Position Sizing**: Kelly fraction=0.5, max position=10%
5. **Execution**: slippage=2bps, commission=5bps, spread=1bps
6. **SL/TP**: Brick-based stop-loss and take-profit
7. **Statistical Rigor**: Bootstrap confidence intervals (circular block), deflated Sharpe ratio
8. **Ranking**: Primary sort by annualized Sharpe ratio, secondary by total P/L
9. **Grid**: brick sizes (per-symbol, 4 levels) × SL (2,3,5) × TP (3,5,7) = 36 combos per symbol
10. **Total Combinations Tested**: 216 (36 × 6 symbols)

## Trading Recommendations

1. **Brick Size Selection**: Stock brick sizes should be ~1-2% of the asset price for optimal signal quality
2. **SL/TP Ratio**: TP > SL generally produces better risk-adjusted returns; aim for 1:2 SL:TP brick ratio
3. **Regime Awareness**: Use HMM regime gate to filter unfavorable market conditions
4. **ETFs vs Single Stocks**: ETFs (SPY/QQQ) have smoother regime transitions; single stocks (TSLA/NVDA) benefit more from adaptive brick sizing
5. **TSLA Specifics**: High-volatility stocks need wider stops (sl_bricks=5); tight stops get whipsawed on daily bars
6. **NVDA Sweet Spot**: Brick=2 with tight SL (2-3) works best — stock is volatile but trending
7. **QQQ Outperformance**: Larger bricks (8) with SL=2-3 work exceptionally well for QQQ — captures tech trends
8. **Re-optimization**: Re-run optimization monthly as market microstructure evolves
9. **Validation**: Always test on out-of-sample data before deploying real capital
10. **Position Sizing**: Use Kelly fraction=0.5 (half-Kelly) for robust position management
