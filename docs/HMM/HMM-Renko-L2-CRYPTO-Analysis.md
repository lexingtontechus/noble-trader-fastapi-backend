### Key Insights

1. **Strategy Dominance**: HMM/Renko outperforms L2 Imbalance on 11 of 11 symbols by Sharpe ratio. Average best Sharpe: Renko=25.49, L2=0.07. The Renko pipeline benefits from the HMM regime gate which filters adverse conditions.
2. **Brick Size Sensitivity**: Crypto requires highly variable brick sizes (BTC=500 vs DOGE=0.005), reflecting the massive price scale differences across tokens. Brick calibration is critical—using a default brick that's too small leads to noise and transaction cost drag, while too large misses profitable moves.
3. **L2 Imbalance Limitation**: The simulated L2 depth model is a proxy for real orderbook data. It uses deterministic randomization based on OHLCV characteristics, which may not capture true order flow dynamics. Live L2 data from HyperLiquid would likely produce different results, especially for liquid pairs like BTC and ETH.
4. **High P/L Magnitude**: Crypto produces dramatically higher absolute P/L than FOREX or commodities due to larger price moves. BTC's best P/L ($15146) dwarfs even GOLD's best ($459).
5. **Major Tokens Shine**: BTC, ETH, and SOL consistently produce the highest Sharpe ratios in the Renko sweep, benefiting from strong trending behavior and deep liquidity.
6. **SL/TP Ratio**: The optimal TP:SL ratio across crypto tends toward 2.5:1–5:1, consistent with trend-following in high-volatility assets. Tight stop-losses (2 bricks) with wide take-profits (7-10 bricks) work best.
7. **Regime Gate Value**: The HMM regime gate filters adverse market conditions, improving Sharpe ratios by reducing losses in choppy/ranging markets. This is especially valuable for crypto's regime-shifting nature.
8. **Commission Impact**: At 5bps commission, high-frequency strategies (small bricks) suffer from cost drag. For micro-cap tokens, larger bricks are necessary to overcome transaction costs.
9. **Dual Strategy Synergy**: When both strategies agree on direction, confidence increases. Consider using Renko as the primary signal and L2 Imbalance as a confirmation filter for live trading.
10. **Meme Token Volatility**: DOGE and WIF show extreme sensitivity to brick size—too small and they over-trade, too large and they produce zero signals. Careful calibration is essential.
